Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using Spendly. This includes expense entries, category names, amounts, dates, and any descriptions you add. We do not collect sensitive financial information such as bank account numbers or credit card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide you with the expense tracking service. We use it to display your expenses, generate summaries, and help you understand your spending patterns. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your expense data is stored securely on our servers. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. You can delete your data at any time by deleting your expenses or your account.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties. We may use third-party service providers to host and maintain our infrastructure, but these providers do not have access to your personal data except as necessary to provide their services to us.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can do this directly through the application settings. If you need assistance, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Your continued use of the service after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us.
